Field discovery

A rep talks into their phone in a parking lot, and the team who gets the shop open receives a record they can actually work from, with the unanswered questions named on it rather than left as an empty field.

Summarising the conversation was the easy half of this. The difficulty is that a CRM holds no opinion about what a complete conversation looks like for this particular kind of shop, so an answer nobody asked for and an answer that came back fine sit on the record looking exactly alike.

What the rep actually said, four hours later

“Kestrel, this was Tuesday I think. Parking was a nightmare. Talked to the manager, Teddy? Ted? They’re on an epi core system, wants better reporting, hates the processing fees. Two lanes, he wants a third. Grabbed lunch after at the taco place. Oh and they rent equipment out the back, trailers and a scissor lift, so that’d need to tie in.”
