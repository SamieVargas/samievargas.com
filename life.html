The Raccoon Invoice

samievargas.com/raccoon ↗

For five nights I slept badly and could not explain it. I blamed podcasts. I cancelled plans. Then I got up early one Wednesday and found a mother raccoon and her babies nesting on my balcony.

The wearable had the whole story before I did. What surprised me was the tail: the raccoons left on May 3 and it took eight more days to get back to baseline. The nervous system does not get the memo.
